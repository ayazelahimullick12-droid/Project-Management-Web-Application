## Background {#background}

Every microfinance branch handles cash every day: loan disbursements, instalment collections and savings deposits. At the end of the day the branch accountant must show that the cash in the vault matches the transactions in the core banking system. Branch accountants, branch managers and area managers are all involved.

## Problem statement {#problem}

Branches reconcile daily cash by hand in Excel. Differences are found days later, take a long time to trace, and sometimes are never explained. Area offices spend hours each week chasing spreadsheets.

## Objectives {#objectives}

1. Reconcile each branch's cash automatically every day against core banking data.
2. Flag differences on the same evening so they can be traced while the day is fresh.
3. Give area managers one view of all open differences in their area.

## Scope {#scope}

**In scope:** daily cash count entry, automatic matching with core banking transactions, difference flags, manager review and area manager follow-up.

**Out of scope:** bank deposits, payroll and changes to the core banking system itself.

**Coverage:** all microfinance branches, rolled out division by division starting with Dhaka.

## Deliverables {#deliverables}

A web app for branches, a matching service, an area manager dashboard, and training material for accountants and managers.

## Timeline and budget {#timeline_budget}

- **Timeline:** eight months, November 2026 to June 2027.
- **Go-live:** Dhaka division by April 2027; all divisions by June 2027.
- **Budget:** BDT 2.4 crore.
- **Funding:** Microfinance Programme operations budget.

## Risks {#risks}

- Core banking data may arrive late in the evening.
- Accountants may keep using Excel in parallel.
- Branches have stable broadband, with 4G backup in some places.
