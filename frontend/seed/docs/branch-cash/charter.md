## Purpose {#purpose}

This charter authorises the Branch Cash Reconciliation project. Branches reconcile daily cash by hand in Excel and differences are found days later. The project will reconcile cash automatically every day and flag differences the same evening.

## Objectives and success criteria {#objectives}

- **OBJ-1:** Reconcile every branch's cash automatically each working day.
- **OBJ-2:** Trace every difference within 2 working days.
- **OBJ-3:** Give area managers one view of open differences.

**Success criteria:** 95% of branches close the day with no unexplained difference; differences are traced within 2 working days.

## Scope {#scope}

- **In scope:** daily cash count entry, automatic matching, difference flags, manager review, area manager follow-up.
- **Out of scope:** bank deposits, payroll, and the core banking system itself.
- **Coverage:** all microfinance branches, division by division starting with Dhaka.

- **Total budget:** BDT 2.4 crore.
- **Funding source:** Microfinance Programme operations budget.

## Governance and roles {#governance}

The Project Manager leads delivery and is the first approver. The Tech head owns the solution. The Head of Microfinance Operations represents the branches. The Director, Finance signs the Charter, BRD and SRS.

## Risks, assumptions and constraints {#risks}

- **Risk:** core banking data arrives late on busy days.
- **Risk:** accountants keep a parallel Excel sheet.
- **Assumption:** every branch has a computer and a working connection at closing time.
