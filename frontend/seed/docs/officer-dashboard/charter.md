## Purpose {#purpose}

Give managers one dashboard to follow each loan officer's portfolio.

## Objectives and success criteria {#objectives}

- **OBJ-1:** Cut loan processing time from 7 working days to 2 working days for at least 90% of applications by spotting delays early.
- **OBJ-2:** Reduce overdue follow-up time by half.

## Budget and funding {#budget}

BDT 70 lakh, Microfinance Programme operations budget.

## Risks, assumptions and constraints {#risks}

Core banking reports must be available by 8 am each day.
