## Background {#background}

Branch and area managers check each loan officer's portfolio by reading several reports from the core banking system.

## Objectives {#objectives}

1. One dashboard per officer with portfolio, collections and overdue loans.
2. Faster decisions on loan approvals and follow-up.

## Scope {#scope}

**In scope:** officer dashboard, branch and area roll-ups. **Out of scope:** changing loan data. **Coverage:** all branches.

## Timeline and budget {#timeline_budget}

Six months from March 2025; budget BDT 70 lakh.
