## Users and stakeholders {#users}

Branch managers, area managers and regional managers.

## Business requirements {#requirements}

- **BR-01:** Show each officer's portfolio and overdue loans. *(OBJ-2)*
- **BR-02:** Show pending loan applications and their age. *(OBJ-1)*

## Reporting needs {#reports}

Branch managers need a daily list of pending loan applications and their age; area managers need a weekly roll-up.
