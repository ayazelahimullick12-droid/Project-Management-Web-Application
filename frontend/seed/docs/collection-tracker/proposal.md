## Background {#background}

Field officers record weekly loan collections in paper registers at courtyard meetings. The registers are typed into the branch system the next day.

## Objectives {#objectives}

1. Record collections on a tablet at the meeting.
2. Get collection data to the branch the same day.

## Scope {#scope}

**In scope:** collection entry, offline storage, sync, branch view. **Out of scope:** loan applications and disbursements. **Coverage:** 80 branches in Dhaka and Mymensingh.

## Timeline and budget {#timeline_budget}

Nine months from January 2024; budget BDT 1.5 crore from the Microfinance Programme.
