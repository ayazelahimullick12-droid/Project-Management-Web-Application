## Purpose {#purpose}

Replace paper collection registers with a tablet app that works offline at courtyard meetings.

## Objectives and success criteria {#objectives}

- **OBJ-1:** Collections reach the branch record on the same day for 90% of meetings.
- **OBJ-2:** Remove double entry at the branch.

## Budget and funding {#budget}

BDT 1.5 crore: BDT 1.2 crore one-off, BDT 30 lakh per year running. Funded by the Microfinance Programme.

## Risks, assumptions and constraints {#risks}

Field officers may resist moving from paper; run paper and app side by side for two weeks at 10 pilot branches, with a field champion in each branch. Many villages have no mobile data.
