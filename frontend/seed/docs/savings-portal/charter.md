## Purpose {#purpose}

This charter authorises the Member Savings Statement Portal: members check their savings and download statements without visiting the branch.

## Objectives and success criteria {#objectives}

- **OBJ-1:** Members can check their savings balance and statement without visiting the branch.
- **OBJ-2:** Reduce statement requests handled by branch staff by 60%.
- **OBJ-3:** Keep member data private.

## Scope {#scope}

- **In scope:** login, balance, 12-month transactions, PDF statements, staff printing, SMS alerts.
- **Out of scope:** loan balances, payments, transfers.
- **Coverage:** Dhaka division branches first.

## Deliverables and milestones {#milestones}

| Milestone | Target |
| --- | --- |
| SRS signed | August 2026 |
| SQA complete | September 2026 |
| UAT complete | October 2026 |
| Live in Dhaka division | November 2026 |

## Budget and funding {#budget}

- **Total budget:** BDT 95 lakh (BDT 80 lakh one-off, BDT 15 lakh per year running).
- **Funding source:** Partner Foundation grant.

## Governance and roles {#governance}

The Project Manager leads delivery; the Tech head owns the solution; the Head of Microfinance Operations represents branches and members; the Director, Finance signs.

## Risks, assumptions and constraints {#risks}

- Shared phones may expose one-time codes.
- Core banking syncs every 30 minutes, so balances can be up to 30 minutes old.
