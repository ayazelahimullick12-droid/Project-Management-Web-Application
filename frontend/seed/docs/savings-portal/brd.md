## Business context {#context}

Members cannot check their savings without visiting the branch. The Charter objectives are self-service access (OBJ-1), fewer staff requests (OBJ-2) and privacy (OBJ-3).

## Users and stakeholders {#users}

- **Members** with a mobile phone.
- **Branch staff** who print statements for members without phones.
- Around 900,000 members in the Dhaka division.

## Current process (as-is) {#current_process}

A member asks the branch for a statement. Staff look up the account in core banking, print the statement and stamp it. This takes 10–15 minutes per request.

## Future process (to-be) {#future_process}

The member logs in with their mobile number and a one-time code, sees their balance and transactions, and downloads a statement. Members without phones ask the branch, where staff print it from the portal in one click.

## Business requirements {#requirements}

- **BR-01:** Members log in with their mobile number and a one-time code. *(OBJ-1, OBJ-3)*
- **BR-02:** Members see their savings balance and the last 12 months of transactions. *(OBJ-1)*
- **BR-03:** Members can download a statement as a PDF. *(OBJ-1)*
- **BR-04:** Branch staff can print a statement for members without phones. *(OBJ-2)*
- **BR-05:** Members get an SMS when a deposit or withdrawal is recorded. *(OBJ-1)*
- **BR-06:** Members trust the statements they see. *(OBJ-3)*

## Reporting needs {#reports}

Monthly number of logins, statements downloaded and statements printed by staff, per branch.

## Assumptions and constraints {#constraints}

Most members have a basic phone that can receive SMS; about half have a smartphone.
