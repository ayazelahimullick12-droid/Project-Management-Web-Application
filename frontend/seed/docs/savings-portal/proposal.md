## Background {#background}

Members ask branch staff for their savings balance and statements several times a year. Each request takes staff time and members often travel to the branch only to ask. Members and branch staff are affected.

## Problem statement {#problem}

Members cannot check their savings without visiting the branch, and branches spend hours each week printing statements by hand.

## Objectives {#objectives}

1. Members can check their savings balance and statement without visiting the branch.
2. Fewer statement requests reach branch staff.
3. Member data stays private.

## Scope {#scope}

**In scope:** member login with a one-time code, balance and 12-month transaction view, PDF statements, staff printing, SMS alerts.

**Out of scope:** loan balances, payments and transfers.

**Coverage:** all branches in the Dhaka division first.

## Deliverables {#deliverables}

A mobile-friendly member portal, a branch staff screen for printing statements, and an SMS alert service.

## Timeline and budget {#timeline_budget}

- **Timeline:** May to November 2026.
- **Go-live:** November 2026.
- **Budget:** BDT 95 lakh.
- **Funding:** Partner Foundation grant.

## Risks {#risks}

- Members share phones, so one-time codes may reach the wrong person.
- Core banking data is synced only every 30 minutes.
