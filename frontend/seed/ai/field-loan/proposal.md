## Background {#background}

BRAC's microfinance field officers collect loan applications from members in their homes and at courtyard meetings. Today every application is written on a paper form and carried back to the branch. This proposal comes from the Microfinance Operations team and affects {{fact:target_users}}

The Technology Unit has been asked to replace the paper form with a tablet app that works where our members live, including villages with little or no mobile data.

## Problem statement {#problem}

{{fact:problem}}

Each lost or incomplete form means a second visit by the field officer, a longer wait for the member, and extra checking work at the branch. Members who need money quickly for their business or household often wait more than a week.

## Objectives {#objectives}

1. **Faster loans:** shorten the time from application to disbursement.
2. **Complete applications:** every submitted application has all required information and documents.
3. **Same-day approval:** {{fact:objectives}}
