## Business requirements {#requirements}

- **BR-01:** Field officers can fill in a loan application on a tablet without an internet connection. *(OBJ-1)*
- **BR-02:** The app captures the member's photo and national ID with each application. *(OBJ-2)*
- **BR-03:** An application cannot be submitted while required fields are missing. *(OBJ-2)*
- **BR-04:** Branch managers approve or return applications in the app. *(OBJ-3)*
- **BR-05:** Loans above BDT 1 lakh go to the area manager for approval. *(OBJ-3)*
- **BR-06:** Managers see a daily list of pending applications per branch. *(OBJ-1)*
