## Purpose {#purpose}

This charter authorises the {{project}} project. {{fact:problem}} The project gives field officers a tablet app to complete loan applications in the field and send them to the branch for approval, even without mobile data.

## Objectives and success criteria {#objectives}

- **OBJ-1:** Reduce loan processing time in the pilot branches.
- **OBJ-2:** Make every submitted loan application complete and checked.
- **OBJ-3:** Let branch managers approve applications on the same day.
